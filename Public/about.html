About Aazad

I am Aazad — a polymath by temperament and a beginner by choice. The name means free, and freedom is the working principle of this site: no single subject, no single method, no schedule except curiosity.

I read mathematics the way some people read novels, and novels the way some people read proofs. I like old star charts, small programs that do one thing well, and questions that sit comfortably in more than one department.

What this place is

The Way of Aazad is a personal intellectual journal. It is not a business, a brand or a feed. It is a handwritten corner of the Internet, made in the spirit of the early personal homepage: expressive, individual, and slightly over-decorated.
